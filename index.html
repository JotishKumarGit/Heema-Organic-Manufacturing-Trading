About Heema Organic

At Heema Organic Manufacturing & Trading, we are dedicated to reviving traditional Indian practices through modern organic innovation. Our mission is to deliver eco-friendly, chemical-free, and sustainable products that support healthier lifestyles and a greener planet.

Who We Are

Heema Organic is a purpose-driven brand rooted in Indian heritage and committed to natural living. We specialize in high-quality, earth-conscious products that serve both spiritual and agricultural needs.

  • Moringo Power: A powerful organic blend derived from moringa and other natural ingredients — ideal for soil enrichment, plant vitality, and organic farming solutions.
  • Cow Dung Powder: 100% natural, sun-dried and finely ground — perfect for use as organic fertilizer, composting, and soil rejuvenation.
  • Cow Dung Cakes: Traditionally handmade and eco-friendly — used for spiritual rituals (havan), natural fuel, and air purification.

Every product we offer reflects our unwavering commitment to purity, sustainability, and the well-being of both people and the planet.
